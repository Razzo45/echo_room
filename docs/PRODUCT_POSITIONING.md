# Echo Room — Product Positioning

**Tagline (product):** Bridge cold corporate networking with warm collaborative storytelling.

**Promise:** Attendees arrive already knowing people — because they co-authored a story together before day one. Organisers get a measurable pre-event funnel and shareable cohort artifacts.

---

## Three product panels (all necessary)

| Panel | Audience | Job |
|-------|----------|-----|
| **Participant** | Attendees | Triple-layer companion (see below) |
| **Organiser** | Event owners | Event ownership: publish events, codes, newsletters/forum, AI quests/rooms, funnel + insights |
| **Admin** | Platform ops | Break-glass: dead rooms, overrides, audit, retention, system settings |

Organiser and admin panels are **not** deprecated. Participant layers do not replace them.

---

## Participant: three layers

1. **Social skeleton** — search, networking requests, real-time IM (event-scoped).
2. **Forum / newsletter** — organiser-authored speakers, panels, updates (“why am I here?”).
3. **Echo Room playspace** — private 1:1 rooms + persistent open rooms; story runtime; gamified profile (level banner, badges, finite arrival-kit quests).

**Dual surface inside the participant app**

- **Event side** (layers 1–2): professional networking + event value.
- **Echo Room side** (layer 3): adult cyberpunk playspace.

**Bridge:** “Play together” invite (primary). Optional organiser toggle to offer a private room on network-accept. Open/matchmaking rooms preserved.

**Forum → rooms:** new posts seed **new** rooms only. In-progress rooms keep immutable content snapshots.

- **B — Echo Room addon:** playspace (+ identity hooks) beside Whova/Swapcard/Luma-class apps.

---

## Engagement ethics (non-negotiable)

- Value before ask (feed/directory work without play).
- Timeboxed missions; async; pause without punishment.
- No streaks, no decaying progress, no variable-reward pull notifications.
- Dice randomness stays **in-session** after consent.
- Finite arrival-kit checklists — not infinite battle passes.
- No default public leaderboard.
- Soft invites; hard stops; “you’re set for the event” exit.

Success = companionship + completed co-plays + artifacts — not time-in-app maximisation.

---

## Differentiation

Others gamify showing up (booths, sessions, points). Echo Room gamifies **arriving already bonded** through shared story play.
